A review of the Programų sistemos bachelor at Vilnius University.

Ratings

Everything is graded on a 0-10 scale.

  • Difficulty How hard the material was to understand and pass.
  • Workload How much time it demanded - lectures, homework, projects.
  • Usefulness How relevant and useful the topics covered are to computer science and software engineering.
  • Quality How well and how engagingly the subject was taught - textbooks, slides, labs, professors.
  • Takeaway How much I personally came away with. Could be low if it was taught badly, or if I already knew the material.
